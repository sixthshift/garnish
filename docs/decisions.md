| 68 | The shopping list is the one screen whose writes queue offline: a tick, an untick and a remove go into `src/lib/outbox.ts` — a `localStorage` queue, coalesced per line, applied to the rendered list at once and flushed in order when `useOnline` turns true or the page regains focus, with a failed entry and everything behind it retained and "N changes waiting" in the header. A recipe save is the opposite — a whole document, replayed over whatever the row has become — so the editor's refusal stands and there is no general outbox to maintain. Coalescing per line means a mind changed twice is one write, not three, and stopping the flush at the first failure keeps a tick and the untick behind it in the order they were made.
